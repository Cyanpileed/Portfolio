About me

As a graduate with a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Universitas Trisakti, I have always been fascinated by how physical systems come to life. Among all the branches of mechanical engineering, my absolute favorites are CAD software and Non-Destructive Testing (NDT). CAD allows me to channel my creativity into detailed digital designs, while NDT takes me out of the office and straight into the field to inspect, analyze, and ensure structural integrity in real-world environments.

What drives me is simple: pure curiosity and a love for building things that actually work. Whether I’m diagnosing engine troubles at DC Motor, trading crypto, or diving into coding and programming, I am always exploring something new. I am also learning German and looking forward to pursuing my career in the DACH countries.

When I’m not knee-deep in hardware or designs, you'll probably find me swimming laps to clear my head or packing a bag to explore a brand-new country and soak in a different culture. For me, whether it's navigating unfamiliar streets abroad or cracking a tough mechanical puzzle, the adventure is all about learning by doing.

Looking ahead, I am eager to pursue opportunities outside Indonesia where I can advance my career in CAD and NDT, elevate my problem-solving skills, and continue doing the things I love on a global stage. Let's build something awesome together!
